Khoroos

(Persian for “rooster”, pronounced kho-ROOS)

An open toolkit for poultry behavior analysis

Khoroos turns poultry-house footage into individual bird trajectories, behavior timelines, and descriptive behavior statistics. Developed at the UT Smart Agriculture Lab.
